Support Privacy Policy

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Botaniq ("we", "us") collects when you use the Botaniq mobile app, how we use it, and the choices you have. Botaniq is a plant care app that helps you identify plants, get AI-assisted health diagnoses, and follow personalized care reminders.

Information we collect

CategoryWhat it's used for
Account info (email, name)Creating and securing your account, sign-in via email, Google, or Apple
Photos you take or uploadIdentifying plants and diagnosing health issues via AI analysis
Location (while app is in use)Providing weather-aware, local care recommendations — only with your permission
Camera & microphoneOnly used when you actively scan a plant or record a video within the app
Subscription & purchase dataProcessing and managing your Botaniq Premium subscription
Usage dataUnderstanding app performance and improving features

Third-party services we use

We rely on trusted third-party providers to operate Botaniq. Data may be shared with them strictly to provide the service:

  • Supabase — backend infrastructure, authentication, and data storage
  • OpenAI — analyzing plant photos and powering the AI Plant Doctor chat
  • RevenueCat — managing subscriptions and in-app purchases
  • Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple — optional authentication methods

These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Data retention & deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from Botaniq's Settings screen inside the app.

Your choices

  • Camera, photo library, location, and microphone permissions can be managed anytime in your device's Settings.
  • You can cancel your subscription anytime via Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your device.
  • You can permanently delete your account and its data directly within the app.
